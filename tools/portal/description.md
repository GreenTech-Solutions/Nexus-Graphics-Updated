**Deprecated: Karu_Kiruna released [Nexus-Graphics](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Nexus-Graphics) for Factorio 2.1 with everything this fork had. Use it instead; this fork gets no more updates.** To move a save: turn off Nexus-Updated, Nexus-Threat-Updated and Nexus-Graphics-Updated, and turn on Nexus, Nexus-Threat and Nexus-Graphics. Buildings and research stay; the shield stabilizers start at tier 1 again, and the instability, the shield and the storm timer start from zero. Outside storms the shield regenerates only with the map setting "Passive shield regeneration".
