It is a stopgap until the original has a 2.1 release; then this fork is deprecated. Source: [GreenTech-Solutions/Nexus-Graphics-Updated](https://github.com/GreenTech-Solutions/Nexus-Graphics-Updated).

Graphics for [Nexus Extended Promethium Endgame (2.1)](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Nexus-Updated). It comes as a dependency of Nexus-Updated; nothing to install on its own.
